01 — About

A short introduction

I'm Hrishikesh. I began coding at 15 because I wanted to build my own video game; the game never quite arrived, but the habit did.

I graduated in information technology and now build AI systems used at enterprise scale, and I still like problems whose answers aren't easy to find online. Away from a screen I farm a small plot and play the sarod, neither of which has a deploy button.

02 — Work

Work, one row at a time

Three stops, newest first. A number of these systems are in production at companies near the top of the Fortune 500; the names stay off the page.

  1. 2024 — 2026 Fractal Analytics Built an enterprise LLM gateway that gives teams one entry point to several model providers, with usage tracking and cost governance, and designed the backend of an enterprise AI catalog.

    The gateway runs on Kubernetes and sits in front of several model providers. It handles millions of daily requests, with routing overhead under 20 ms at p95, and keeps per-team usage and cost visible to the people paying for it.

    The catalog's backend indexes internal models and datasets, so teams can see what already exists before building it again. The client is a large U.S. healthcare enterprise, which is as specific as I can get.

    Several projects I worked on are in production at some of the largest Fortune 500 companies, including a few in the top five. That is the part I'm quietly proud of, and the reason the names stay out of this page.

    • Python

    • PostgreSQL
    • Redis
    • OpenTelemetry
  2. 2023 — 2024 Avananta Innovations Built NLP-heavy conversational AI systems at an early-stage startup and spent a lot of time on how quickly they responded.

    Most of the work was NLP: intent classification, entity extraction, dialogue state, and the evaluation harnesses that kept them honest. Performance work made responses 15% faster.

    The team was small, so the work moved between design, code, deployment and the unglamorous parts of making a demo hold up in production.
